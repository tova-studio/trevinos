What’s on at Trevinos

Celebrations, live music & the big game

Trevinos Restaurant & Bar, Riccarton, Christchurch is a perfect venue for special occasions. Choose the restaurant, the bar, the outdoor garden areas or the conference and function rooms, and we can cater for all your requirements.

Functions

Special Occasions & Group Bookings

From family celebrations to work functions, there’s a space to suit. Try our new menu, Christmas menu, wine list, brunch or tapas menu, set menus or function catering. The gaming lounge and accommodation at Christchurch City Park Motels are on site.

Book today on (03) 343 5378

Every Friday

Live Music – Friday Nights

We have live music almost every Friday night of the year. It’s generally contemporary, and start times vary around other events, such as rugby on the big screen. Vague As Bros, one of the top live acts in Christchurch, play here regularly and are not to be missed.

Freedom Friday drinks specials run from 5–8pm for that after-work party.

Sport & gaming

Live Sports on the Big Screen

Watch all the games on the big-screen TV in the bar. Summer means cricket. In winter, rugby is number one: the mighty Crusaders are a crowd favourite, and the All Blacks always get top support from our patrons.

Have a punt in our gaming room, with 17 gaming machines on site and a TAB over the road.

About Trevinos

Inside & Outside Dining

The building faces nor-west with a bush-clad outlook, and outdoor seating for dining, coffee or a casual drink. Indoors, the restaurant seats up to 60 people, with extra room in the bar area when needed. In summer, the outdoor area becomes a hive of activity.

Relax around the open fire on those colder days

Our bar has an open gas fire with an Oamaru stone surround. With comfortable lounge seating, it’s the perfect place to meet friends for coffee or a drink.

  • Indoor restaurant seating for up to 60 people
  • Bar lounge with an open gas fire and Oamaru stone surround
  • Outside area with a gas fire, TV and water feature
  • Outdoor seating at tables or bar leaners

Good to know

Your questions, answered

When is Trevinos open?

We’re open 7 days a week from 9am to 1am, and earlier by arrangement. We open every day except Christmas Day.

Where is Trevinos in Christchurch?

We’re at 22 Riccarton Rd, Christchurch, on the corner of Riccarton Road and Mona Vale Avenue in Riccarton.

Can I book a group or a special occasion?

Yes. You can choose the restaurant, the bar, the outdoor garden areas or our conference and function rooms, with set menus and function catering available. Phone (03) 343 5378 to book.

Which night is live music on?

Friday. Live music plays almost every Friday night of the year, and Freedom Friday drinks specials run from 5–8pm.

Can I watch the rugby or cricket at Trevinos?

Yes. Sport shows on the big-screen TV in the bar: cricket in summer, and rugby in winter, including the Crusaders and the All Blacks.

Is there outdoor seating and accommodation?

Yes. Our outside area has a gas fire, TV and water feature, with tables and bar leaners. Accommodation at Christchurch City Park Motels is on site.
